The short version

I started as a singer-songwriter, became a producer and mastering engineer for Sony, Warner and Disney, and co-launched the first business podcast for the recording industry. It went viral, and I ended up coaching award winners on how to build teams and careers that last.

During COVID I had a breakdown, was hospitalized, and was diagnosed with PTSD from being raped by a priest as a child. Therapy worked. Then I did the next scary thing: I went to the Ohio Statehouse and said so, out loud, and started writing laws. The Scout’s Honor Law passed in Ohio in 2023. Three more states followed.

Along the way I built the first agentic AI in the audio industry, and then Pete the Pigeon: Ohio’s Statehouse AI, every bill, vote, hearing, dollar and lobbyist filing, with every answer tied to the public record. Today I build movements for employee owners, survivors and award-winning creatives, and I help offices on both sides of the aisle put AI to work without getting burned.
