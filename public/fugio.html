Track record.

Done, and in progress.

  • The Scout's Honor Law

    I helped author it and led the survivor advocacy. The Ohio Senate passed it unanimously, giving 1,911 Boy Scout abuse survivors in Ohio a path to full settlements. Alabama, Indiana and Iowa then passed their own versions. That's 5,000+ survivors, and hundreds of millions of dollars in settlements.

  • Spousal rape exemption, gone

    Ohio had one. Almost 40 years of back room deals kept it there and kept Ohio out of the modern world. I worked on the bill that removed it. Gov. DeWine signed it.

  • A grooming law for Ohio
